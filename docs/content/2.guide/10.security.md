title: Security
description: What the module keeps on the server, what reaches the browser, and what you must protect.
navigation:
  icon: i-lucide-shield
---

- **Credentials never reach the browser.** Provider configuration lives in
  private `runtimeConfig.openFeature`. Only `flagRouteBase` is public.
- **The client sees only what you allow.** The batch endpoint returns
  `publicFlags` and nothing else.
- **Browser context is untrusted input.** The checksum detects corruption; it
  does not authenticate anyone. Base access decisions on your server session,
  and pass trusted identity through
  [`getFeatureFlagContext`](/guide/server-evaluation#trusted-context).
- **Signed-in users' email addresses are sent as traits** to your server and on
  to your providers. Check that this fits your privacy policy and your vendors'
  data processing terms.

::warning
The diagnostics route has no built-in authentication, and it lists flags that
are not in `publicFlags`. Protect it with your own server middleware before
exposing your app publicly. See [Endpoints](/reference/endpoints).
::

## Reporting a vulnerability

Use
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/DevelopmentSprint/nuxt-openfeature/security/advisories/new),
not a public issue.
